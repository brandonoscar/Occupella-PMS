# Code of conduct

Occupella PMS is built by people who manage property, people who write software, and people who
do both. Everyone who takes part in its issues, discussions and pull requests is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, with newcomers above all;
- argue about the code and the books, never about the person;
- keep real people's data out of every report, screenshot and log;
- accept a maintainer's decision on what the project includes, and say so politely if you
  disagree.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, discrimination, sharing someone's private information, and
sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to the maintainers with GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or
issue, or by opening a private security advisory if it involves someone's data
([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Reports are read by the maintainers only and kept private.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock a discussion, or block someone from the
repository, in proportion to what happened and whether it continues. Decisions are explained to
the person they concern.
